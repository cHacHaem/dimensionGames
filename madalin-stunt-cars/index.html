How To Play

Use WASD or arrow keys to control your vehicle and C to change your view. Change its color to suit your style and preferences. Choose from three thrilling maps, each offering unique challenges and environments. Experience realistic physics that bring your stunts to life, and push the limits with daring maneuvers and high-speed action. Get ready for an adrenaline-filled adventure!

The Code

Madalin Stunt Cars was created using Unity, a powerful and versatile game engine widely used for developing both 2D and 3D games. Unity provides developers with tools to create immersive experiences, including physics simulations, rendering, and scripting capabilities. Learn more about Unity at Unity's official website.
