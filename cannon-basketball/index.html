How To Play

Cannon Basketball is a fun and engaging game where players use a cannon to shoot basketballs into hoops. The objective is to aim carefully and adjust the power of the cannon to score points by getting the basketball into the hoop. The game challenges players with different levels, obstacles, and varying hoop positions.

To play, use the controls provided in the game interface to adjust the angle and power of the cannon. Once you're ready, fire the basketball and try to score!
