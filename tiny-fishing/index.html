How To Play

Tiny Fishing is a relaxing and addictive fishing game where you cast your line into the water and try to catch as many fish as you can in a single drop. The deeper your line goes, the more valuable and exotic the fish become—but you'll need to upgrade your gear to reach them. Earn coins from each catch to buy better rods, increase your line length, and hook multiple fish at once. With its simple controls, colorful graphics, and satisfying progression, Tiny Fishing is the perfect casual game to unwind and see just how deep you can go.

The Code

Tiny Fishing is a lightweight HTML5 game built entirely with plain JavaScript and the HTML5 canvas API—no frameworks or engines. The core logic lives in a single file (TinyFishing.js) which handles rendering the fishing line, fish, and physics. It's designed to run in any modern browser, focusing on simplicity and smooth performance .
