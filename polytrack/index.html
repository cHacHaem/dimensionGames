How To Play

Poly Track is a single-player racing game where you drive a low-poly car to the finish line through creative and challenging levels. The game also allows you to compete against your own previous records by following your ghost, which represents your best performance.

Use WASD or arrow keys to control your vehicle, avoid obstacles, and reach the finish line!

The Code

Poly Track was created using various tools and technologies: Three.js for 3D rendering, Bullet Physics Engine for game physics, Blender for 3D modeling, GIMP for 2D asset creation, and Audacity for audio editing.
