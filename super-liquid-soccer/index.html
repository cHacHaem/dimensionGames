How To Play

Super Liquid Soccer is a fun and fast-paced soccer game that combines simple graphics with deep, skill-based gameplay. Control your team through regular matches or take on the ultimate challenge in the World Cup—can you outscore the competition and claim the trophy? Use WASD to move, J to shoot or head the ball, M to pass, L to chip or cross, and P for a powerful boost. With quick action and creative plays, every match feels like a highlight reel waiting to happen!

The Code

Super Liquid Soccer was made with the Godot Engine, a powerful open-source game engine known for its lightweight design and flexible 2D and 3D capabilities. Godot allows developers to build games efficiently with a user-friendly interface and a built-in scripting language called GDScript.
