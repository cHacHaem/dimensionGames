How To Play

Geometry Dash is a fun and challenging rhythm-based platformer game where you control a square that jumps and flies through various levels filled with spikes, obstacles, and thrilling music. Click space, the screen, or the up arrow to jump. The objective is to navigate through each level by timing your jumps perfectly to avoid hazards and reach the end. As you progress, the levels become more complex and require quick reflexes and precise timing. With its catchy tunes and addictive gameplay, Geometry Dash offers an exciting experience for players of all ages.

The Code

This version of Geometry Dash was created using Unity, a powerful and versatile game engine widely used for developing both 2D and 3D games. Unity provides developers with tools to create immersive experiences, including physics simulations, rendering, and scripting capabilities. Learn more about Unity at Unity's official website.
