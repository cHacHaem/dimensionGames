How To Play

Super Hot is a thrilling first-person shooter game where time moves only when you move. Your objective is to eliminate all enemies in each level while avoiding getting hit. Strategically plan your moves and shots to survive and progress through increasingly challenging levels.

Use WASD or arrow keys to move, space to jump, and mouse to aim and shoot.

The Code

Super Hot was built using Unity, a powerful game development platform. The game leverages Unity's physics engine and scripting capabilities to create the unique time-manipulation mechanics that define Super Hot's gameplay experience.
