How To Play

Drive Mad is an exciting 3D driving game where players navigate their car through challenging obstacle courses. Use the arrow keys or A/D to control your vehicle, mastering precision and timing to overcome ramps, gaps, and other tricky terrain. With creative levels and satisfying physics, Drive Mad offers a thrilling experience that tests your driving skills and creativity. Can you conquer all the levels and become the ultimate driver?
