How To Play

Customize your car and take on the perilous highway, where you'll need to skillfully navigate through traffic at high speeds. Choose from 4 thrilling game modes: Time Attack, Speed Bomb, One-Way, and Two-Way, each offering unique challenges to test your driving prowess. Push your limits and dominate the road!

The Code

Highway Racer was created using Unity, a powerful and versatile game engine widely used for developing both 2D and 3D games. Unity provides developers with tools to create immersive experiences, including physics simulations, rendering, and scripting capabilities. Learn more about Unity at Unity's official website.
