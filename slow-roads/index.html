How To Play

Slow Roads is an endless driving game that emphasizes relaxation and exploration over speed and competition. The objective is to drive along a never-ending road, enjoying the scenery and the calming atmosphere. There are no time limits or scores to achieve; the goal is simply to experience the joy of slow, mindful driving.

The Code

The game is built using JavaScript and WebGL for rendering the graphics. It utilizes procedural generation techniques to create an endless road and environment, ensuring that each playthrough offers a unique experience. The game's physics engine simulates realistic car handling, allowing players to navigate the road at their own pace.
