How To Play

Stickman Hook is a fun and challenging physics-based game where you control a stickman swinging through various levels using a grappling hook. Click or hold the spacebar to shoot the hook at surfaces, then swing and release to navigate through obstacles and reach the finish line. Timing and precision are key as you try to avoid falling or crashing into hazards. Enjoy the thrill of swinging and mastering each level!
