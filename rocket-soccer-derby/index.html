How To Play

Rocket Soccer Derby is a fast-paced, physics driven online and offline car sports game where you drive rocket boosted vehicles on a soccer field and smash a giant ball into your opponent's goal using intuitive controls—WASD or arrow keys to steer, Shift for nitro boost, Spacebar to jump, and F to lock the camera on the ball. You can play solo against AI or team up in 2 or 3 player modes, climbing from beginner to expert leagues while customizing your car's appearance and performance. Mastering aerial maneuvers, timing boosts and jumps, and positioning yourself defensively or offensively are key to outscoring opponents in short, explosive matches.

The Code

Rocket Soccer Derby was created using Unity, a powerful and versatile game engine widely used for developing both 2D and 3D games. Unity provides developers with tools to create immersive experiences, including physics simulations, rendering, and scripting capabilities. Learn more about Unity at Unity's official website.
