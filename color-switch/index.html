How To Play

Color Switch is an exciting and challenging game where you must navigate a colored ball through various obstacles that match the ball's color. The objective is to pass through as many obstacles as possible without hitting a different color. Collect stars along the way to increase your score and unlock new levels and features.

Click or tap to make the ball jump. Time your jumps carefully to match the colors of the obstacles!

The Code

Color Switch was developed using Unity, a versatile game development platform. The game utilizes Unity's physics engine and scripting capabilities to create smooth gameplay and engaging mechanics that keep players coming back for more.
