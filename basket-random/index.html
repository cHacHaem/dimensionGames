How To Play

Basket Random is a fun and chaotic 2-player basketball game where players compete to score points. Each player controls their character using simple controls: Player 1 uses the "W" key, and Player 2 uses the "Up Arrow" key. The objective is to outscore your opponent by making baskets while dealing with random physics and unpredictable gameplay elements.
