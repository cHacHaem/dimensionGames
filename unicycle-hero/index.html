How To Play

Unicycle Hero is a fun and challenging game where you control a unicyclist competing to throw, flip, and launch objects as far as possible. Use the arrow keys to balance and move, and press the space bar to interact with the object—whether that's hitting, throwing, or flipping it. The better your performance, the more points you earn! Collect coins to upgrade your unicyclist's abilities and appearance. Aim to beat your high score and become the ultimate Unicycle Hero!

The Code

Unicycle Hero was created using Unity, a powerful and versatile game engine widely used for developing both 2D and 3D games. Unity provides developers with tools to create immersive experiences, including physics simulations, rendering, and scripting capabilities. Learn more about Unity at Unity's official website.
